Cookie Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

1. Current cookie use

This portfolio does not intentionally set advertising, analytics, marketing, or other non-essential cookies. Because there are no non-essential cookies being used by the site, there is currently no cookie-consent banner or consent wall.

2. Local storage

The light/dark theme preference is saved in your browser's local storage so the site can remember your display preference. Local storage is stored by the browser on your device and is not a cookie. The site does not use this value for advertising or behavioural tracking.

3. Analytics and tracking

No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising pixel, session-recording service, fingerprinting script, or similar analytics tracker is intentionally installed in the current portfolio.

4. Third-party content

The site avoids embedded third-party media where practical. Links to external services such as social networks and project sites are ordinary outbound links. Their handling of cookies and personal data is governed by their own policies once you visit them.

5. Changes

If analytics, advertising, embedded media, authentication, payments, or other technologies that use cookies or similar identifiers are added later, this policy will be updated and the site will implement any consent mechanism required by applicable law before non-essential tracking is activated.
