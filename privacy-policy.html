Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hrishabh Sharma handles information in connection with this portfolio website. The site is designed to collect as little personal data as reasonably necessary.

1. Who operates this website

Operator: Hrishabh Sharma
Location: Solan, Himachal Pradesh, India
Email: sharmahrishabh800@gmail.com

2. What this website collects

This website does not have an account system, advertising system, analytics platform, or database-backed contact form. It does not intentionally collect sensitive personal data.

  • When you email me: if you choose to use your email application to contact me, the information in that email is received in my email account. I use it to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, discuss or deliver requested work.
  • Theme preference: the site may store your light/dark theme preference in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to me by the website.
  • Contact form: the optional contact form on this site does not submit information to a website server. It prepares an email in your own email application. Your email provider then handles the message.

3. Cookies

This site does not intentionally use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. The site uses browser local storage for the theme preference; local storage is different from a cookie and is not transmitted to this site.

4. Third-party services and links

The portfolio contains links to third-party websites such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, and client/project websites. When you leave this site, those services operate under their own privacy practices. This site does not control how those services process your information.

The site is intended to avoid unnecessary third-party embedded content and remote tracking scripts. Its static hosting may still involve technical processing by the hosting provider as part of serving web pages.

5. Why information is used

Information voluntarily sent to me is used only for legitimate communication, responding to enquiries, discussing projects, providing requested services, maintaining business records where reasonably necessary, and complying with legal obligations.

6. Data minimisation and retention

Please do not send passwords, financial information, government ID numbers, health information, or other sensitive information through this portfolio. I will seek to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, for an agreed business relationship, or where retention is required by law.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access to, correction of, withdrawal of consent for, or deletion of personal data, subject to lawful exceptions. 8. Security

I use reasonable measures appropriate to the limited nature of the information handled through this website. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children

This portfolio is not directed at children and does not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

This policy may be updated when the website, services, or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its update date.
