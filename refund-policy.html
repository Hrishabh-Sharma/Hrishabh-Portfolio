Refund Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

1. No online checkout on this website

This portfolio currently does not sell products or take payments through an online checkout. This policy therefore does not create a refund right for a purchase that the website does not offer.

2. Freelance projects

For a paid freelance project, the scope, price, deposit, milestones, cancellation terms, deliverables, revisions, ownership, and refund treatment should be agreed in writing before work begins. A project-specific written agreement takes precedence over this general policy to the extent permitted by law.

3. Deposits and work already performed

Where a project agreement provides for an advance or milestone payment, the agreement should state whether that payment is refundable. Amounts relating to work already performed, third-party costs, domain/hosting purchases, or other non-recoverable expenses may be treated as specified in the written agreement, subject always to mandatory legal rights.

4. Cancellation

If you need to cancel a project, contact me as soon as possible at sharmahrishabh800@gmail.com . I will review the project's status and the applicable written agreement.

5. Defective or materially non-conforming work

If delivered work materially fails to match the agreed scope, please report the issue promptly so it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, corrected. Any statutory rights you may have are not waived by this policy.

6. Third-party charges

Domain registrations, hosting, paid software, stock assets, fonts, plugins, or other third-party purchases may have separate refund rules controlled by the relevant provider. Those terms are not controlled by this website.

7. Contact

Refund or cancellation questions should be sent to sharmahrishabh800@gmail.com .
